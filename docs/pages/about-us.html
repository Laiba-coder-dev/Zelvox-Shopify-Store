We test what we sell

Zelvox started with a simple frustration: gadgets that looked great online and disappointed in real life. So we began testing everything ourselves before recommending it.

Today the range covers smartphones, audio, wearables, smart-home devices and accessories. We still check build quality, battery life and connectivity on every product before it earns a listing — and we still answer support questions ourselves.

If a product cannot survive our own everyday use, it does not go on sale.
